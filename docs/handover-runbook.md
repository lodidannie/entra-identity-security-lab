## Proposed operating procedure

1. At cycle start, verify review scope, reviewer availability, eligible membership coverage and schedule.
2. During the window, monitor completion and follow up on unanswered items.
3. At close, validate decisions and record exceptions. Treat nonresponse as unresolved, not approved.
4. Have the assigned operator apply validated removals manually; check the specific role, eligibility or resource membership targeted.
5. Verify effective access, including other access paths. Retain the evidence in approved internal systems.
6. Confirm next occurrence and add new groups to coverage using the supported review configuration approach.

New-group discovery and coverage reporting are potential future automation, not an implemented capability in this package. Do not assume newly created PIM groups enter an existing review automatically. Notification recipients are not presumed to own all operational tasks.
