# Design tradeoffs and lessons

- Permanent eligibility removes recurring assignment work but needs continued recertification.
- Eight-hour activation supports daily operations but grants longer elevated exposure than short task-based activation.
- No approval prioritizes responsiveness; justification, enforced MFA, monitoring and reviews should support accountability. Monitoring implementation is not evidenced here.
- Require MFA does not establish a phishing-resistant authentication strength.
- Both eligible and active group members matter because potential access remains a governance concern.
- Role assignments and group memberships are complementary review surfaces.
- Manual application makes initial outcomes controllable but needs a named operator and completion tracking.
- No change on nonresponse protects operational continuity while leaving unresolved access in place.
- Running reviews establish process initiation; completed certification and verified removals establish outcomes.
- Sanitized documentation can demonstrate design judgment without exposing employees, tenant identifiers or internal evidence.

These are design interpretations of the documented configuration, not measured production improvements.
